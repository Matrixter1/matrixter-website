Participation and privacy enquiries

When you contact us

The website contact form sends your name, email address and message to Netlify Forms. Email contact@matrixter.com if you prefer to write directly.

When you answer on Pulse

Guests can explore the feed. Submitting a vote requires signing in. The web app sends Supabase your account ID, the question, your answer and a beta verification flag. A scale response can include an optional reason. Keep sensitive personal details out of free-text answers.

Pulse summarises responses for each question. Its web beta uses a simulated verification flow. Treat the flag as a beta account label. People choose which questions to answer, and a difference between groups tells you only how those participants responded.

Ask about your information

For questions about access, correction or deletion, write to contact@matrixter.com. Say whether your enquiry concerns the website contact form or a Pulse account. Please leave identity documents and passwords out of your email.
